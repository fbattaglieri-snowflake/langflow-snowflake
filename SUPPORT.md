# Support

Community best-effort support is provided through this repository's issues.
There is no response-time or maintenance commitment. Neither Snowflake nor
Langflow support is responsible for this deployment kit. For upstream issues,
prepare a minimal reproduction on a supported unmodified upstream release.

Before filing an issue include the public commit/image version, redacted error,
deployment method and synthetic reproduction. Never attach your config, tokens,
flow exports, account hostname, user data or logs without reviewing them.
